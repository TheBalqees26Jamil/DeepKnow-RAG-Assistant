Batch Normalization in Deep Learning

==================================================
1. Introduction
==================================================

Batch Normalization is a technique used to stabilize and speed up training in deep neural networks.

It works by normalizing the inputs of each layer so that they have a consistent distribution during training.

==================================================
2. Why Batch Normalization is Important
==================================================

During training, the distribution of inputs to each layer can change, making learning unstable.

This problem is known as:

Internal Covariate Shift

Batch Normalization helps by:

- Stabilizing learning
- Allowing higher learning rates
- Reducing sensitivity to initialization
- Improving overall performance

==================================================
3. How Batch Normalization Works
==================================================

For a mini-batch of activations:

Step 1: Compute mean
Step 2: Compute variance
Step 3: Normalize
Step 4: Scale and shift

--------------------------------------------------
Formula:
--------------------------------------------------

x̂ = (x − μ) / sqrt(σ² + ε)

Then:

y = γ x̂ + β

Where:
- μ = batch mean
- σ² = batch variance
- ε = small constant for numerical stability
- γ = learnable scale parameter
- β = learnable shift parameter

==================================================
4. Where Batch Normalization is Used
==================================================

Batch Normalization is typically applied:

- After linear transformation (Wx + b)
- Before or after activation function (depends on architecture)

Common in:

- CNNs
- Deep feedforward networks
- Modern deep architectures (ResNet, etc.)

==================================================
5. Advantages of Batch Normalization
==================================================

- Faster convergence
- More stable training
- Reduces vanishing/exploding gradient issues
- Allows higher learning rates
- Acts as a mild regularizer

==================================================
6. Disadvantages of Batch Normalization
==================================================

- Not ideal for very small batch sizes
- Adds computation overhead
- Different behavior during training vs inference

==================================================
7. Training vs Inference Behavior
==================================================

During training:
- Uses batch statistics (mean and variance of current batch)

During inference:
- Uses running averages of mean and variance

==================================================
8. Key Insights
==================================================

- Batch Normalization improves training stability
- It is widely used in modern deep learning architectures
- It allows deeper networks to train effectively
- It reduces sensitivity to initialization and learning rate

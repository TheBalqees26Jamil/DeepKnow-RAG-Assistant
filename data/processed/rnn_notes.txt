Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)

RNNs are designed for sequential data such as text, time series, and speech.

Key Idea:
RNNs have memory, meaning they consider previous inputs when processing current input.

Problem:
Vanishing gradient problem makes it hard to learn long-term dependencies.

Solution:
LSTM and GRU were introduced to solve this issue.

Applications:
- Language modeling
- Speech recognition
- Time series prediction
